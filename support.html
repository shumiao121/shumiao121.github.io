Subscriptions

How do I cancel or change my subscription? In Kaleap: Settings → Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Kaleap. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. After cancelling, Kaleap Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

I paid for Kaleap Pro but the app doesn't show it. Open Settings → Film roll → See membership and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I get a refund? Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

Photos

Why is there a Kaleap mark on my photos? Photos you save to your photo library or share from Kaleap carry a small Kaleap wordmark in the corner. The copy inside the Kaleap album stays unmarked.

Can I use Kaleap's features on photos I already have? Yes — import a photo into the Kaleap album. HD and Erase work on imported photos.

What do I need? An iPhone with iOS 16 or later. Enlarge person needs iOS 17 or later.

Privacy

How do I stop photos from going to the cloud? Settings → Storage & general → Cloud processing. After that, Kaleap works entirely on your phone and the cloud features stop offering to run.

How do I delete my data? Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone. To delete the records on our server, email us the Anonymous ID shown in Settings → Storage & general.

Camera, microphone or location isn't working. Check that Kaleap is allowed in your iPhone's Settings → Kaleap.
